# Home

## Purpose

The front door of Larchwick Coffee Roasters, a fictional specialty coffee roaster. It tells a
visitor what Larchwick sells and sends them on to the coffees, the story behind the roastery, or
a subscription enquiry.

## Sections, in order

1. **Skip link** — hidden until a keyboard user presses Tab; "Skip to content" jumps past the
   menu to the page content.
2. **Header** — the Larchwick name in the brand's serif lettering and the menu: Home, About,
   Services, Products, Blog, FAQ and Contact. The current page is underlined in the menu. The
   header scrolls away with the page, as on the old site. On phones the menu collapses into a
   menu button, and the opened menu lies over the page instead of pushing it down.
3. **Hero** — a dark coffee-brown band with "Specialty coffee roastery · Est. 2019", the headline
   "Fresh coffee, roasted in small batches", a short paragraph about roasting days and shipping,
   two buttons ("Shop our coffees" and "Our story") and three promises with a tick: "Roasted to
   order", "Free EU shipping over €40" and "Pause or cancel subscriptions any time". This is the
   only top-level heading on the page.
4. **Features** — three centred items, each with a coffee-cup icon: "Roasted to order", "Fully
   traceable" and "Brewing help", each with one sentence.
5. **Subscription call to action** — on a light cream band: "Try a subscription", "Fresh coffee
   every 2 or 4 weeks. Save 10%, pause any time." and an "Ask about subscriptions" button.
6. **Footer** — a dark band with the Larchwick name, a one-sentence description, the label "Demo
   project — fictional business", the menu links, the contact details (email, wholesale email
   and phone), the opening hours and a copyright line.

## Search and sharing

- Page title: "Larchwick Coffee Roasters | Fresh Small-Batch Coffee", and the same description
  as the old site, word for word.
- Shared links show the Larchwick coffee-bean preview image.
- Search engines are told who Larchwick is (name, website, email and phone).

## Key interactions

- The menu links open the matching page
- On a phone, the menu button opens and closes the menu
- "Shop our coffees" opens the Products page; "Our story" opens the About page
- "Ask about subscriptions" opens the Contact page
- The email addresses in the footer open the visitor's mail app; the phone number starts a call
  on a phone
